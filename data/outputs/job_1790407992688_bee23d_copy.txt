# Prestige Parklane KIADB Phase‑2 – STRR‑Devanahalli Campaign Package

## 1. Campaign Positioning

**Concept:** *“Timeless Elegance in the Heart of North Bangalore”*

Prestige Parklane KIADB Phase‑2 (STRR‑Devanahalli) is positioned as a benchmark of refined living within Bangalore’s most coveted North Bangalore corridor. This campaign targets affluent professionals, emerging families, and discerning investors who seek a seamless blend of urban convenience, architectural excellence, and curated luxury. The visual language—warm neutral palettes, organic textures, and cinematic lighting—evokes a serene yet aspirational lifestyle, aligning with the “Premium & Cinematic” DNA.

**Core Message:** *Exclusive access to a premier residential enclave where heritage meets modern sophistication.*

---

## 2. Creative Direction Summary

| Element | Specification |
|---------|---------------|
| **Visual Mood** | Premium • Cinematic • Warm Neutrals + Accent Gold/Brass |
| **Aspect Ratio** | 16:9 (1024×1024) |
| **Duration** | 6 seconds |
| **Primary Palette** | Gold/Yellow, Dark Grey/Black, White |
| **Lighting** | Natural daylight with soft directional glow; warm undertones |
| **Composition** | Centralized symmetry for logo; panoramic wide‑shot of the development; layered depth with foreground greenery |
| **Typography** | Elegant Serif for “Prestige”; Clean Sans‑Serif for body & tagline |
| **Key Assets** | Rendered architectural visuals, warm‑toned interiors, subtle bird motif (growth symbolism) |

## 4. Creative Execution Notes

- **Image Spec:** 1024×1024 (16:9) – cinematic still capturing the parkland vista with a hint of the building silhouette, warm golden hour lighting, and subtle depth layers (foreground trees, midground courtyard, background skyline).  
- **Video Option (if required):** 6‑second looped cinematic cut – opens on close‑up of textured interior (linen, wood grain), pans to exterior architecture bathed in soft light, ends on logo with “Enquire Now” overlay.  
- **Tone:** Sophisticated, aspirational, serene – never playful. Maintain premium color temperature throughout.  
- **Factual Constraints:** All claims must align with the brief – no exaggerated price figures beyond “competitive” phrasing; all locations (North Bangalore, KIADB, Phase‑2, STRR‑Devanahalli) must be accurately represented.
